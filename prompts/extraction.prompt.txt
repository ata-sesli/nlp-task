COVERAGE AND CONTEXT
Return one page_extractions entry for each physical PDF page in
{{PAGE_NUMBERS}}, in ascending order, assessing all three fields on
every supplied page. Do not renumber pages within the group. Use
physical source-page numbers, not printed document page numbers.
Classification labels and groups are provisional context for identifying
roles; they are not evidence that a value appears on a page.

FIELD MEANINGS
- borrower_name: Names explicitly identifying the borrower, including
  printed names, readable names under borrower signatures, affiant
  names in notary clauses, and borrower aliases established by a name
  affidavit. Exclude names of sellers, lenders, notaries, settlement
  agents, and loan originators. Do not extract generic role words such
  as "Borrower" or "the undersigned" as names.
- property_address: The address of the property securing the loan,
  including any unit number. Exclude borrower mailing addresses and
  addresses of sellers, lenders, or settlement agents.
- loan_number: An identifier explicitly referring to the loan. Exclude
  MIN, settlement file numbers, parcel numbers, form codes, and licensing
  identifiers. Preserve leading zeros and conflicting numbers.

OBSERVATIONS AND VISIBLE EVIDENCE
Record each qualifying occurrence once, even when the same value appears
elsewhere on the page. Repeated views of the same occurrence are not
separate occurrences. Retain competing values and readable struck-through
names. Do not assume conflicting names are equivalent aliases unless visible evidence
establishes that relationship. A readable name remains extractable when
adjacent dates or other text are unclear.

Every value, quotation, field label, and factual note must be supported
by visible evidence on its physical source page. Never reproduce hidden
or covered text. A note may describe a covered region, but must not
assert its contents or the number of concealed occurrences. Cross-page
context may explain a role; it cannot supply missing text or move a value
or quotation onto another page. Never copy a value between pages or
infer that it appears on every page of the document.

For each observation:

  Transcribe internal spaces in personal names literally, even within an

Preserve disagreements and uncertainty. Do not invent text, extract
